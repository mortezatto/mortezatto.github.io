## Overview

IoTrain is an EU-funded Erasmus+ consortium project focused on building IoT research and
teaching capacity across partner institutions. As research staff at IASBS under Prof. Dr.-Ing.
Mahdi Bohlouli, I contributed to the project's research and training deliverables across a
multi-country collaboration spanning Germany, Romania, and Iraq.

## Role

My involvement spanned both the research and knowledge-transfer sides of the consortium: applied
IoT and intelligent-systems research work, plus direct participation in the project's
international training events — summer and winter schools designed to build IoT teaching
capacity across the partner network.

## Outcomes

Beyond the research contributions, this project was a sustained exercise in cross-border
collaboration — coordinating deliverables, teaching materials, and quality standards across
institutions with different academic cultures and constraints. It's also where the Research &
Technology Achievements Provincial Exhibition Award (Zanjan, 2022) came from, recognizing the
consortium's applied IoT work.
